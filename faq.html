Frequently Asked Questions

Get instant answers regarding delivery times, minimum orders, design pre-press support, payment methods, print quality, and file specifications. Operating Mon-Sat 10:00 am to 8:00 pm.

⏱️ What are your Operating Hours and Delivery Times?

▼
Shree Printing Press operates from Mon-Sat 10:00 am to 8:00 pm. We offer three production speeds to fit your schedule:
  • • Standard Production: 3 to 5 business days with standard courier shipping.
  • • Express Priority: 2 business days expedited turnaround.
  • • Supercharge Express: Same-Day / 24-Hour emergency dispatch for urgent trade show deadlines.

📦 What is the Minimum Order quantity required?

▼
For our Digital On-Demand Press, there are virtually no minimum order limits! You can print as few as 10 brochures, 50 business cards, or 1 retractable vinyl banner. For high-volume Commercial Offset Runs, minimum orders start at 500 units to unlock maximum bulk cost savings.

🎨 Do you offer Design Support and artwork proofing?

▼
Yes! Every order includes complimentary pre-press artwork verification by our expert team. We check your files for proper bleed margins (0.125"), color mode (CMYK), and image resolution (300+ DPI). We also offer full custom graphic design services if you need artwork created from scratch.

💳 What Payment Methods do you accept?

▼
We accept all major Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Bank Wire Transfers, and corporate Purchase Orders (P.O. net-30 terms for approved account holders).

⭐ How do you ensure Printing Quality and color accuracy?

▼
Our press facility is ISO 12647-2 certified. We utilize in-line spectrophotometer color scanners on our 8-color Heidelberg Speedmaster offset presses and HP Indigo digital units. This guarantees 100% Pantone match precision, zero ink bleeding, and crisp 2400 DPI text reproduction.

📂 Which File Formats should I upload for printing?

▼
We recommend press-ready PDF/X-1a files with fonts embedded or converted to outlines. We also accept Adobe Illustrator (.AI), Photoshop (.PSD), EPS, TIFF, and high-resolution PNG files (minimum 300 DPI in CMYK color mode).
